A2A Protocol — Agent2Agent Protocol

A2A is an open standard, originated by Google and donated to the Linux Foundation, that lets AI agents built on different frameworks (LangGraph, CrewAI, Google ADK, Genkit, and more) discover each other's capabilities and collaborate on tasks securely.
